[arXiv:2601.22943v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22943v2) · [OpenReview WtINGEMjTB](https://openreview.net/forum?id=WtINGEMjTB)

Thank you to Xiang Wu, Rong-Hua Li, Xunkai Li, Kangfei Zhao, Hongchao Qin, and
Guoren Wang for developing and sharing the STPGC concepts, algorithms, source

audit possible.

This repository is a transparent companion record. It distinguishes the one
small source-faithful toy fixture that has actually run from the six live
paper claims that remain unverified at paper scale. It does not imply author
review, endorsement, or responsibility for this independent implementation.
